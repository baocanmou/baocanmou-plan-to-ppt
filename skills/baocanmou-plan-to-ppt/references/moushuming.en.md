# MouShuMing: the method used in this Skill

**MouShuMing® Brand Positioning and Design Model**. Author: **Yi Huiting**. Brand: **BaoCanMou**. This application summary follows the model's 4.0 content version; it is not the original model text and does not assert separate registration for version 4.0. This summary has the distinct scope stated in [NOTICE](../NOTICE.md).

## Preserve the relationship

**Zhi is the values center. Mission explains why the brand exists; vision explains where it intends to go. The center guides three modules.**

- **Mou — positioning:** target market, audience and differentiation. Answer what you are, how you differ and what supports that difference.
- **Shu — design:** translate positioning into visual, language and experience elements that people can perceive, remember and trust. This is broader than decorating slides.
- **Ming — communication:** bring the resulting expression and materials to the audience through channels, content and cadence, then collect responses and review them. This is broader than posting advertising.

Zhi → Mou → Shu → Ming may describe work order, but Zhi is not a fourth parallel execution module. A model diagram must preserve a center that guides the other three.

## Read, decide, express

| Area | Look for | Decision and presentation |
|---|---|---|
| Zhi | Founder statements, mission, vision, confirmed principles | Explain beliefs and tradeoffs; unknown founder beliefs stay unknown |
| Mou | Assessment, source card, competitors, user statements, product evidence | Establish category, audience, difference and proof; no unresearched claim of a market gap |
| Shu | Confirmed positioning, perception problems, existing assets | Link each design task to positioning and a use scene |
| Ming | Available resources, resulting materials, actual user responses | Connect audience, channel, content, cadence, feedback, owner and budget |

Example: **Mou:** test office afternoon demand for cold-brew tea (hypothesis). **Shu:** prioritize category, flavor and preparation information, then a relaxed feel (proposal). **Ming:** use the same explanation in office sampling and related content; record comprehension and intent to try (plan).

Renaming three chapters does not establish these connections.

## Inputs and scope

Brand strategy, design and communication tasks use these judgments within the user's chosen structure. Do not add an unnecessary theory lecture. Nonbrand reports, training and faithful formatting do not require this model; respect a user's explicit choice not to apply it.

A full brand assignment builds on the Brand Assessment Report from BaoCanMou's five-part diagnosis method, the brand source card and founder interviews. Reuse existing inputs. Without them, produce a scoped partial plan or explicit hypothesis and identify what needs evidence. Do not claim a completed diagnosis, verified positioning or confirmed values.

The three-act proposal method handles the telling of contracted project results, while MouShuMing organizes content. Do not automatically use the former in an unconfirmed engagement or redefine it as a sales-closing model.

## Working rules

1. Keep values, mission and vision separate. Quote the founder where possible; missing vision invites a question, not an invented slogan.
2. Address all positioning questions. Call unsupported differences hypotheses. Do not convert a chosen scene into an exclusive advantage.
3. State each design task's positioning basis, perception goal and use scene. Distinguish creative copy from interview quotations. Label concept imagery.
4. Consider existing touchpoints and resources before proposing paid expansion. Production, people and maintenance have costs. Without testimonials, propose collecting feedback rather than fabricating it.
5. Review feedback against positioning, perception tasks and brand principles, not only views. Claims of sales, enquiries or reach need evidence.

The plan's optional methodology record links these elements. Automated checks validate declared relationships, not commercial soundness. Keep internal IDs and logs in working files, while customers see clear proposal content.
